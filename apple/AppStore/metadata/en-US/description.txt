Paperloom is a research workbench built around your papers: collect them, read and annotate them, take notes, and cite them as you write. Every PDF, highlight, note and drawing stays attached to its paper, so you can move from one to the other in a tap.

COLLECT
• Add a paper by DOI, arXiv ID, PubMed ID or ISBN, and the details are filled in for you.
• Drop in a PDF and Paperloom recognises the paper from its first pages, even when the page is a scan.
• Search Crossref, OpenAlex, arXiv, PubMed and Semantic Scholar at once, plus library catalogues such as the Library of Congress, and add results straight to your library.
• Import RIS, BibTeX, EndNote XML, PubMed and Web of Science files, or open them in Paperloom from Files, Mail and other apps.

READ
• A fast PDF reader with highlights, underlines, comments, ink, shapes, text boxes, stamps and signatures, all listed beside the page.
• Recognise Text turns scanned pages into searchable, selectable text, on your device.
• Search Everything finds words across every paper's full text, your notes and your annotations.

THINK AND WRITE
• Markdown notes with live preview, [[links]], backlinks, tags, templates and a graph of how notes connect.
• Mind maps, hand-drawn pages with Apple Pencil and diagrams, inside your notes or on their own.
• Ask the AI about a paper or your whole library, with your own API key; answers cite their sources.

CITE
• Copy a citation or a bibliography in APA, IEEE, GB/T 7714, Chicago, MLA and many more styles.
• Cite in Word Document: write [@key] markers in a .docx or .odt and get a copy with formatted citations and a reference list.

YOUR DATA, YOUR CHOICE
• Your library and notes live on your devices. Sync and back up to iCloud, WebDAV, S3 or Seafile storage you choose.
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

WORKS WITH THE TOOLS YOU KNOW
• Runs many Obsidian and Zotero 7 plugins in a sandbox.
• Spotlight, Shortcuts and Handoff for papers and notes.

Available in English, 简体中文, 繁體中文, Français, 日本語 and Русский.
